Cross the target at the second you promised. IP Inbound turns a target, an initial point and a time on target into live course and speed guidance, from the countdown on the ground to the moment you pass overhead.

THE PROBLEM

You are lead of a formation over a ceremony, or you are being scored on putting a flour bomb on a mock target. Either way you are handed three things: a target, a time on target (TOT), and an initial point (IP) or run-in direction.

The rest is on you: pick a run-in speed, plan the arrival over the IP, and keep adjusting as the winds and the timeline move. When a band or a pyrotechnics display is keyed to your pass, ten seconds off is wrong.

IP Inbound does that arithmetic continuously, in flight, so you can fly.

PLAN

Place the target on the map, by name, or as latitude/longitude, UTM or MGRS. Set the run-in bearing, distance and ground speed, and IP Inbound works the TOT backwards to find your time over the IP. No final TOT yet? Set it later; it can change in the air.

FLY

ON THE GROUND, a countdown to TOT keeps engine start, taxi and departure honest.

PRE-IP, the course deviation indicator gives direct-to guidance to the IP, and speed guidance tells you to speed up or slow down to make your time over it. If you are very early, it becomes a countdown to your push time.

OUT OF TIME FOR THE IP, the title turns red and reads P.POS → Target, and guidance goes direct to the target from your present position.

ON THE RUN-IN, the indicator switches to desired-track guidance along the IP-to-target bearing, and speed guidance drives you to cross at exactly TOT.

PAST THE TARGET, it reports the pass in seconds early or late. Set several targets with sequential TOTs and chain them with one tap after each pass.

Timing includes the turn to the IP and the turn onto the run-in, flown as level 30°-banked turns. Throw in a 360 or an S-turn and the estimate still holds.

ALSO

• Apple Watch repeats the indicator, the countdown and speed guidance, for when the phone is kneeboarded and your eyes are outside. A complication keeps the countdown on the watch face.
• A Live Activity keeps the countdown and your run-in figures on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
• Ask Siri "Time on target in IP Inbound" to hear how long you have.
• Targets sync over iCloud, so you can build the list on the iPad and fly it from the iPhone. They turn up in Spotlight, too.
• Knots, MPH or KPH; local or Zulu time.

IP Inbound is free and open source, with no ads, no subscription and no tracking. Your position is processed on the device and never transmitted.

IP Inbound is a planning and situational-awareness aid. It is not certified for navigation. The pilot in command remains responsible for the safe conduct of the flight.
